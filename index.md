  <section id="research" class="section">
    <div class="section-heading">
      <p class="eyebrow">Research</p>
      <h2>Questions across genomes, pathways, and adaptation</h2>
      <p>My work combines chromosome-scale genome assembly, comparative and population genomics, and multi-omics to connect evolutionary change with biological function.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card-grid">
      <article class="research-card">
        <span class="card-number">01</span>
        <h3>Natural rubber genomics</h3>
        <p>Evolution and regulation of natural rubber biosynthesis in <em>Taraxacum kok-saghyz</em>, linking genomic variation, carbon allocation, laticifer biology, and rubber yield.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="research-card">
        <span class="card-number">02</span>
        <h3>Complete plant genomes</h3>
        <p>Haplotype-resolved, telomere-to-telomere assemblies used to resolve structural variation, repetitive regions, centromeres, and haplotype-specific regulation.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="research-card">
        <span class="card-number">03</span>
        <h3>Regulatory genomics</h3>
        <p>Integration of population resequencing, GWAS, RNA-seq, ATAC-seq, and allele-specific expression to dissect complex traits.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="research-card">
        <span class="card-number">04</span>
        <h3>Parasitic plant evolution</h3>
        <p>Comparative genomics of parasitic lifestyles, host adaptation, gene-family evolution, and race-specific virulence in <em>Orobanche cumana</em>.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </section>
