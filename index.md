## Research Interests

### Natural Rubber Genomics

I study the evolution and regulation of natural rubber biosynthesis in rubber-producing dandelions, with the aim of connecting genomic variation, gene regulation, and rubber-related traits.

### Haplotype-Resolved T2T Genomes

I use long-read sequencing and chromosome conformation capture technologies to construct haplotype-resolved, telomere-to-telomere plant genomes and investigate structural variation, repetitive regions, centromeres, and haplotype-specific regulation.

### Population and Regulatory Genomics

I integrate population resequencing, genome-wide association studies, RNA-seq, ATAC-seq, and allele-specific expression to identify genomic and regulatory mechanisms underlying complex traits.

### Parasitic Plant Evolution

I use comparative genomics to investigate the evolution of parasitic lifestyles, host adaptation, gene-family changes, and race-specific virulence in parasitic plants.
